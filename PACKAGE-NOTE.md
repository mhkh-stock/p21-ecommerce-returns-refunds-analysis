# P21 — Package Note (Fiverr-ready)

**Gig title:** I will analyse your e-commerce returns and show you what they cost and what to fix first

**What the client gets:** a returns and refunds analysis for
their online store — return rate overall, by product category,
by sales channel, by product and by month, why customers send
items back (ranked by count and by money lost), what refunds
plus return shipping cost in total (gross − refunded = net
revenue, gross − money lost = revenue kept), the refund vs
replacement split, the top problem products, and four
prioritised fix-first actions. Delivered as a 2-page PDF report
plus a live Excel workbook they can re-run on next month's
orders themselves.

## Tiers (Hashir's band)

| Tier | Price | Delivery | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $20 | 2 days | The 2-page PDF report on up to 500 orders: overall return rate, money lost (refunded + return shipping), top return reason, worst category and worst product, the 4 recommendations |
| Standard | $65 | 3 days | Everything in Basic + up to 2,000 orders, return rates by category, channel, product and month, reasons ranked by money lost, refund vs replacement split, the live Excel workbook with formulas |
| Premium | $140 | 5 days | Everything in Standard + up to 10,000 orders, a returns-reduction plan for the top 3 problem products with size-chart and listing fixes, a reusable returns-analysis template for their own future exports, 1 revision round |

**What's NOT included:** handling the returns or talking to
customers (they run their own returns desk — quoted separately
if they need the process written); changing their listings or
product photos (this service shows them which listings to fix);
a live dashboard that updates itself (this service is a report +
a workbook they re-run — a live dashboard is a different gig).

**Delivery time note:** counts from the day the client uploads
their orders export, not from order placement.

**Proof this service works:** this repo's sample — PakBazaar,
1,200 orders over 6 months, 217 returns (18.08%), gross revenue
PKR 4,203,195, refunded PKR 564,528, return shipping PKR 51,500,
money lost PKR 616,028 (14.66% of gross), Wrong size found as the
top return reason (47 returns, PKR 157,156 lost), Fashion found
as the worst category (30.17%, PKR 317,659 lost), Daraz found as
the worst channel (21.30%), Women's Lawn 3pc Suit found as the
worst product by rate (33.33%) and Running Sneakers as the worst
by money lost (PKR 105,378), March found as the worst month by
rate (23.73%), 326 automated checks passing, and a 2-page PDF
the buyer sees before ordering.
