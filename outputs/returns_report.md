## 1. The headline — what do returns cost PakBazaar?

Of 1,200 orders, 217 came back (18.08%). Refunds took PKR 564,528 off gross revenue (net revenue PKR 3,638,667), and return shipping added PKR 51,500 on top — so returns cost **PKR 616,028 in total**, 14.66% of everything the store sold. Of the 217 returns, 150 were refunded (69.12%) and 67 were replaced (30.88%). The worst category is **Fashion (30.17%)**, the worst channel **Daraz (21.3%)**, and the worst month **2026-03 (23.73%)**.

## 5. Refund vs replacement

150 of 217 returns were refunded (69.12%) and 67 were replaced (30.88%). Refunds are where the money goes: every refunded order gives the full order value back, while a replacement only costs the return shipping. Defective and wrong-item orders are usually replaced; changed-mind orders are usually refunded.

## 8. Four actions — fix first, in this order

1. **Fix sizing first — it is the biggest leak.** Wrong size is the top return reason: 47 of 217 returns (21.66%), costing PKR 157,156 (25.51% of all money lost). Fashion is the worst category (30.17% return rate, PKR 317,659 lost) and Women's Lawn 3pc Suit is the worst product by rate (33.33%, 32 of 96 orders). Publish real measurements and a size chart on the Fashion listings before spending on anything else.
2. **Tighten the Daraz channel.** Daraz has the highest return rate at 21.3% (82 of 385 orders), against Facebook at 14.67%, and lost PKR 212,039. Check that its listing photos, sizes and descriptions match what is actually shipped — the gap against the Website channel is a listing problem, not a customer problem.
3. **Quality-check before dispatch, starting with Electronics.** Defective/damaged (32 returns, PKR 50,337 lost) plus wrong item sent (25 returns, PKR 38,590 lost) cost PKR 88,927 together — every one of those is a return the store caused. Running Sneakers alone lost PKR 105,378 (27 returns). A one-minute check before sealing the box removes most of it.
4. **Plan for the sale spike before the next one.** 2026-03 had the highest return rate of the half year (23.73%, 56 returns) and 2026-04 lost the most money (PKR 156,761). Sale months bring the volume — and the returns. Staff the returns desk and tighten size and quality checks before the next sale, not after it.
